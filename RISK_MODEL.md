# Risk Model

Risk is intentionally separated from license.

## License Risk

License risk answers: what must be checked before study, integration, distribution, hosted-service use, or practical adoption?

It is represented by the license decision label: permissive with notices, use with obligations, source-release on distribution, hosted-service source-release risk, restricted/non-commercial, no-license monitor-only, or manual review.

## Project Risk

Project risk answers: how risky is it to rely on, study closely, or prioritize this project as an opportunity?

It excludes license risk to avoid double-counting.

Project risk is based on:

- Maintenance risk: archived, disabled, stale, or not recently pushed.
- Hype risk: abnormal star/fork imbalance or very young project with sudden attention.
- Maturity risk: very young repositories are less proven.
- Metadata risk: missing description/topics or forked repository.

## Display Labels

- Stable / 稳健: 0-14
- Watch / 需观察: 15-34
- Volatile / 高波动: 35-59
- Avoid first / 避险优先: 60-100

## Overall Action Risk

Internal ranking still uses an overall action risk that blends project risk and license risk. This affects opportunity and actionability scores, but the UI displays project risk and license risk separately.
