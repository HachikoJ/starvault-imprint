# Open Source Opportunity Taxonomy

This taxonomy classifies repositories by the most useful learning, tracking, practical-inspiration, and opportunity-review context, not by programming language alone.

## Categories

1. AI Products & Agents
   - Agents, RAG, chatbots, MCP, multimodal, prompt workflows, autonomous assistants.
   - Usually high momentum and strong applied-value signals, but also noisy and hype-prone.

2. Developer Productivity & AI Coding
   - CLI, SDK, IDE, code review, testing, docs, CI, coding agents, terminal tools.
   - Strong fit for developer workflows, automation ideas, and internal productivity patterns.

3. SaaS & Business Systems
   - Admin, CRM, billing, auth, workflow, forms, ticketing, internal tools.
   - Good source of operational product patterns, workflow design, and service architecture references.

4. Data, Analytics & Knowledge
   - ETL, analytics, BI, vector search, knowledge graphs, datasets, retrieval systems.
   - Often valuable as infrastructure or enterprise workflows.

5. Infra, Cloud & Platform Engineering
   - Kubernetes, Docker, deployment, observability, tracing, logging, Terraform, SRE.
   - Useful but practical adoption often requires trust, support, and deep technical capability.

6. Security, Privacy & Compliance
   - Scanners, secrets, SAST, IAM, privacy, LLM security, red-team tools, incident response.
   - High willingness to pay, but high correctness and liability expectations.

7. Frontend, Design & Creative Tools
   - UI kits, design systems, prototyping, canvas, image/video tools, editors.
   - Good for polished tooling, creator workflows, and interaction-pattern inspiration.

8. Consumer Productivity & Personal Tools
   - Notes, browser tools, local-first apps, bookmarks, PDF, calendars, personal knowledge.
   - Good for personal use and indie products; moat usually depends on UX and distribution.

9. Commerce, Growth & Content
   - Ecommerce, checkout, CMS, SEO, marketing, newsletter, content, creator tools.
   - Close to real usage scenarios and useful for content, growth, and workflow inspiration.

10. Vertical & Domain Applications
    - Finance, education, healthcare, legal, robotics, IoT, science, geospatial.
    - Commercial value can be high, but domain compliance and expertise matter.

11. Systems, Runtime & Edge
    - Compilers, runtimes, OS, kernels, Wasm, embedded, cache, queues, protocols.
    - Important technical assets, but usually harder to turn into quick products.

12. Learning, Lists & Research Assets
    - Awesome lists, tutorials, courses, benchmarks, leaderboards, papers.
    - Useful for market intelligence, reference learning, and tracking emerging directions.

13. Other
    - Fallback when there is not enough signal for confident classification.
